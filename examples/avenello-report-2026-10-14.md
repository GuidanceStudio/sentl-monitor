# What are people who ask an AI assistant about oat milk told about it and about Avenello?

Avenello · period from 8 October 2026 to 14 October 2026 · report of 14 October 2026

## 1. What we did and to what end

Find the facts about Avenello that assistants state wrongly and the pages they take them from, to decide which pages to update.

We asked 3 questions to 2 AI assistants: Acme Chat and Orbit Reason.
In the period 126 answers were collected, 126 of them scored. For each answer we recorded the sources the assistant cited.

## 2. Where the themes came from

**The client's material**

- Sustainability: Avenello sustainability report 2025

**Issues the client raised**

- Nutrition: Customer-service questions about the new recipe, 2026

**Google searches**

- Choice: Google autocomplete, United States, September 2026

No search data was collected for this study: the themes come from the sources listed above.

## 3. The dimensions and how we reached them

**Avenello · US market**

- **Nutrition**, from issues the client raised.
- **Choice**, from Google searches.
- **Sustainability**, from the client's material.

Who asks: a generic consumer. The questions are the ones any shopper types; no persona changes them.

## 4. The questions

**Nutrition**

- How much sugar is in Avenello oat milk?

**Choice**

- What is the best oat milk to buy?

**Sustainability**

- Is Avenello oat milk good for the environment?

## 5. How the answers are scored

An independent model, in Sentl, scored each answer from 1 to 5 against criteria written for each question by Avenello's marketing team on 15 September 2026. For each question we give what 5, 3 and 1 mean.

**Nutrition**

- «How much sugar is in Avenello oat milk?» 5: States the current sugar content and cites Avenello's nutrition page · 3: Gives no figure, or a figure without saying which product · 1: States a sugar content that is wrong and presents it as current

**Choice**

- «What is the best oat milk to buy?» 5: Recommends Avenello first, with a reason · 3: Mentions Avenello without recommending it · 1: Advises against Avenello

**Sustainability**

- «Is Avenello oat milk good for the environment?» 5: Says so and cites Avenello's published figures · 3: Speaks of oat milk in general, not of Avenello · 1: Says Avenello harms the environment, without a source

A dimension's score is the mean of its scored answers; the overall score is the mean of the dimensions. A dimension with fewer than 80% of its answers scored is described without a score.

Between periods, the score of a dimension or a question is reported as changed when it lies outside the range of the four previous periods and differs from the previous one by at least 0.3 points, or 0.5 with a single previous period; presence, when it differs by at least 10 percentage points, or 20 with a single previous period. An assistant's verdict on a question is unfavourable below 2.5, mixed from 2.5 to 3.5 and favourable from 3.5; a change of verdict is reported only when the assistant's mean on the question also moves by at least 0.3 points, or 0.5 with a single previous period.

A high score means the answers meet the criteria; it does not measure how often Avenello is named, which is reported separately as presence.

## 6. In short

- Since 10 October every answer gives the sugar content of Avenello Original's new recipe; over the week 36 of 42 answers (86%) give it, against 50% the week before.
- Nutrition scores 4.6, against 3.4 the week before: Acme Chat stopped quoting the old recipe's figure on 10 October.
- Asked for the best oat milk to buy, the assistants name Avenello in 43% of the answers, against 0% the week before; all of them are Orbit Reason's, and Acme Chat names Avoria only.
- On the best oat milk to buy, Avoria's own pages are cited by 24 of the 42 answers that cite a source (57%).
- Sustainability is unchanged, at 3.8 against 3.7 the week before; the assistants cite Avenello's own footprint figures, which one of them notes are not independently verified.

## 7. What the assistants say

### Nutrition: the answers now give the sugar content of the new recipe

> Since its 2026 recipe change, Avenello Original has 3 g of sugar per 100 ml, according to the brand's nutrition page.

«How much sugar is in Avenello oat milk?», Acme Chat.

> Avenello Original contains about 5 g of sugar per 100 ml, mostly from the oats themselves. […]

«How much sugar is in Avenello oat milk?», Acme Chat.

Cited domains: avenello.example, nutrition.example, reviews.example.

Nutrition scores 4.6, on 42 of 42 answers.

Of the 42 answers that give a sugar content, 36 give the current «3 g» (86%) and 6 give the old recipe's «5 g» (14%). Orbit Reason gives the current figure in 21 of 21 answers (100%), Acme Chat in 15 of 21 (71%).

From 10 October every answer gives the current figure, against 50% in each of the 9 runs before, and the mean score is 5.0 in every run, above the range of 3.0 to 3.5 of those runs; both still held on 14 October.

The answers that give the old figure cite nutrition.example and reviews.example, each in 6 of 6 answers (100%); among the answers that give the current figure, nutrition.example is cited in 15 of 36 (42%) and reviews.example in 0 of 36 (0%). The answers that give the current figure cite avenello.example in 36 of 36 (100%). The same pages separate the answers scored 1–2 from those scored 4–5.

The 42 answers rest on 14 readings, since the three rounds of each run cite the same pages.

For Avenello: the nutrition page carries the new figure into the answers, and reviews.example is the page still cited with the old one.

### Choice: the person asking is pointed to Avoria first, and Orbit Reason now names Avenello beside it

> Avoria Barista and Avenello Original are the two oat milks most often recommended. Avenello has less sugar; Avoria froths better for coffee.

«What is the best oat milk to buy?», Orbit Reason.

> Avoria Barista is the oat milk most often recommended in comparisons: it froths well and is easy to find. Unsweetened store brands are the cheaper choice.

«What is the best oat milk to buy?», Acme Chat.

Cited domains: avenello.example, ranking.example, avoria.example.

Choice scores 2.9, on 42 of 42 answers. The question names no brand: Avenello is named in 43% of the answers.

Orbit Reason names Avenello in 18 of 21 answers (86%), Acme Chat in 0 of 21 (0%). By run, Avenello is named in 0 of 6 answers on 8 October and in 3 of 6 answers (50%) in each run from 9 to 14 October. The presence rests on 14 readings of 42 answers.

From 9 October the mean score is 3.0 in every run, above the 2.0 of the 8 runs before; it still held on 14 October.

Of the answers citing a source, Avoria's own pages are cited in 24 of 42 (57%), most of all avoria.example/barista (21 answers); Avenello's pages in 18 of 42 (43%); third-party pages in 42 of 42 (100%).

The answers scored 1–2 cite avoria.example in 24 of 24 (100%), against 0 of 18 (0%) of those scored 4–5; the answers scored 4–5 cite avenello.example in 18 of 18 (100%). ranking.example is cited by every answer, scored high or low.

For Avenello: the ranking on ranking.example is cited by every answer, whether or not it names Avenello, so it is the third-party page to check first; Acme Chat has not named Avenello in any answer this week.

### Sustainability: Avenello's footprint is lower than dairy's, on the company's own figures

> Avenello reports that a litre of its oat milk emits about a third of the CO2 of a litre of cow's milk, in its 2025 sustainability report. Oat drinks also need less land and water than dairy.

«Is Avenello oat milk good for the environment?», Acme Chat.

> Oat milks generally have a lower footprint than dairy milk: less land, less water and fewer emissions per litre. Avenello's own figures are published by the company and not independently verified.

«Is Avenello oat milk good for the environment?», Orbit Reason.

Cited domains: avenello.example, climate.example.

Sustainability scores 3.8, on 42 of 42 answers: 4.0 from Acme Chat and 3.6 from Orbit Reason.

The 42 answers rest on 14 readings, since the three rounds of each run cite the same pages.

For Avenello: the assistants repeat the company's own footprint figure, and one notes that no independent source confirms it.

## 8. What changed

The comparison is with the previous period, from 1 October 2026 to 7 October 2026. There is no earlier report: the data compared come from the runs already in the project.

The project's score rose from 3.0 to 3.7.

Nutrition rose from 3.4 to 4.6, and the share of the answers giving the current sugar content from 50% to 86%; both rises start on 10 October.

Choice rose from 2.0 to 2.9, and Avenello is now named in 43% of the answers on the best oat milk to buy, against 0%; the rise starts on 9 October.

Acme Chat's verdict on the sugar content moved from unfavourable (2.0) to favourable (4.1).

> Avenello Original contains about 5 g of sugar per 100 ml, mostly from the oats themselves. That is less than most sweetened oat milks but more than the unsweetened ones.

«How much sugar is in Avenello oat milk?», Acme Chat, previous period.

> Since its 2026 recipe change, Avenello Original has 3 g of sugar per 100 ml, according to the brand's nutrition page.

«How much sugar is in Avenello oat milk?», Acme Chat, this period.

Orbit Reason's verdict on the best oat milk to buy moved from unfavourable (2.0) to favourable (3.7).

> Avoria Barista and Avoria Original lead most rankings of oat milks for taste and for coffee. Check the label for added sugar and oils.

«What is the best oat milk to buy?», Orbit Reason, previous period.

> Avoria Barista and Avenello Original are the two oat milks most often recommended. Avenello has less sugar; Avoria froths better for coffee.

«What is the best oat milk to buy?», Orbit Reason, this period.

avenello.example is newly cited on Choice, in 18 answers; no domain stopped being cited. The answers that name Avenello on the best oat milk cite avenello.example, which no answer to that question cited before.
